Light for customers, dark for operators

One system, two grounds. A person signing into their bank gets the light theme; an operator reading a decision log gets the dark one. Same tokens, same type, same components — only the ground and the tuning of each colour change.

Severity

No red, no amber. Escalation reads as distance travelled away from green — through cyan and indigo into plum. Severity is always an outlined chip in mono; the accent is always a solid control. They can never be confused for each other.

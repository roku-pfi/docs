## Prometheus (per-pod scrape, after kubernetes_sd)

Queried ~3 min into/after the Job (`histogram_quantile` over a 2m rate):

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| PDP pods `up` | 5 |
| `sum(rba_decisions_total)` | 83249 |
| action mix | 100% `REAUTHENTICATE` (novel `load-user-*`, banking bands) |
| p95 `/risk/evaluate` | **0.194 s** |
| p99 `/risk/evaluate` | **0.241 s** |

p95 is at the top of the plan's ~100–200 ms PDP budget under this laptop
load; p99 is above it. That is expected with 24 concurrent writers into one
Postgres and Freeman inline — the HPA adds replicas, it does not shrink
per-request DB time.

## Takeaways

- The HPA is not decorative: CPU 9% → 850% of request moved replicas **2 → 5**.
- Scraping the PDP **Service** under-counts once replicas > 1; Endpoints SD is
  required (ADR-0021).
- Decision mix under synthetic load is not a production prior — every user is
  new, so Freeman + banking policy yields `REAUTHENTICATE`. The dashboard still
  shows the mix the live path emits.

## Caveats

- Single-node k3d on an M2 Pro; numbers are a **local demo**, not a capacity
  claim.
- metrics-server CPU is the HPA input; Grafana HPA panels come from
  kube-state-metrics. No cAdvisor panel.
- Scale-down uses a 300s stabilization window — replicas stay high for ~5 min
  after the Job ends.

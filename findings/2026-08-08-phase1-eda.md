## Takeaways feeding Step 4 (features)

- Lean on faithful per-user consistency: `ip/asn/country/device_type/os/browser
  _seen_before`.
- Behavioral: `user_login_count`, `seconds_since_last_login`, `unusual_login_hour`,
  `failed_logins_recent`.
- Drop `rtt_deviation`; treat geo distance / `impossible_travel` as optional
  low-weight (missingness + synthetic geo).

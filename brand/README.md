# RokuSec — logo files

Vector redraw of the chosen concept
(`material_sources/logos/RokuSec Blue Lock Logo (1).png`). Generated by
[`design/brand/build.py`](../../design/brand/build.py); do not edit these files by
hand — change the script and rebuild:

```bash
python design/brand/build.py      # needs fonttools, uharfbuzz; rsvg-convert for PNGs
```

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `rokusec-logo.svg` | Primary lockup (mark over wordmark). Informe cover, title slides. |
| `rokusec-logo-horizontal.svg` | Headers and navigation bars on light grounds (hosted login). |
| `rokusec-logo-horizontal-inverse.svg` | Same, on dark grounds (operator console). |

## The mark

A padlock whose open shackle and round body together draw the numeral **6**:
*roku* (六) is "six" in Japanese. The lock is drawn **open**, which is the
product's claim: a usual login should open without an extra step, and the lock
only closes when the context is unusual. The keyhole stays at the centre of the
body: the decision is taken at the point of entry, at login.

## Construction

- Geometry measured off the source PNG and kept in its 1254 px pixel space
  (ring centre 628, 646; outer r 191, inner r 107.5; shackle 78 px wide on an
  r 118.75 arc). Numbers live at the top of `build.py`.
- Wordmark: **Sora 700** (SIL Open Font License 1.1, `design/brand/fonts/OFL.txt`),
  converted to outlines, so no file depends on the font being installed.
  "Roku" in navy, "Sec" in the brand blue: the two halves of the name, product
  and purpose.
- Colour: shackle sky → blue, ring blue → navy along the same diagonal, so light
  appears to come from the top right. The inverse variant lifts every stop rather
  than swapping one ink; the navy side would vanish on the console's `#0D1017`.

**Open point:** the UIs' current accent (`#4C5FE0`, indigo, ADR-0030) is not the
logo blue. The UI rebuild should move the RokuSec accent to the logo's blues;
Roku Bank gets its own, unrelated palette.

## Known limits

- At 16 px the keyhole fills in. 32 px and up are fine. If a 16 px icon is ever
  needed, draw a dedicated one (no keyhole, thicker ring) rather than scaling.
